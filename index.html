Appendix — theory and model backbone

Static reference. Numbers here are design parameters and calibration results, not today's forecast. Code: macro/, tools/, journal/ in the Trading repo.

A1 · Thailand CPI nowcast

How TPSO builds the index

Compiler: Trade Policy & Strategy Office (TPSO), Ministry of Commerce — not the central bank. Elementary aggregates use a Dutot index (ratio of arithmetic-mean prices); aggregation above that is modified Laspeyres with fixed weights. Base 2023 = 100 (B.E. 2566), live since January 2025. Basket of 464 items, 7 groups, 52 provinces. Collection frequency is the key to a nowcast: fuel daily, fresh food weekly, everything else monthly, rent six-monthly. No seasonal adjustment, never revised — the first print is final. Roughly 26% of the basket is administered or price-controlled. Core = headline excluding raw food and energy (≈69.7% of the basket).

Known limits

  • Fuel is the edge; food recovers ~half the signal; core cannot be beaten with pre-print data.
  • Electricity's effective household price depends on the consumption block TPSO does not publish — subsidies modelled from announcements.
  • Consensus is sourced from public calendars unless refreshed from the Bloomberg ECO survey.

A2 · US CPI / PCE nowcast (v0)

Bottom-up by CPI component on approximate Dec-2025 relative importances. Energy = gasoline share (3.4% of headline) × month-to-date weekly retail gasoline change vs prior-month average (≈1:1 pass-through) plus the rest of energy at persistence; every other component = 3-month average m/m (persistence). Headline m/m = Σ w·m/m normalised to covered weight; y/y from the index base effect. Core PCE m/m ≈ 0.80 × core CPI m/m + 0.02 (crude mapping; PPI not wired). Status is "insufficient data" until at least two weeks of target-month gasoline exist. Not yet wired: Cleveland Fed nowcast, Manheim used cars, PPI, a consensus feed. Expect it to track consensus except when energy moves a lot.

A3 · Central-bank member agents (Fed, BOJ, BoT)

Idea

One agent per committee member, deciding independently from (i) a sourced dossier — role, term, stance score from the last six months of statements and votes, stated reaction function, recent statements with URLs, voting record; (ii) a shared datapack — market pricing, recent prints, nowcasts, news, the last statement; (iii) at most three searches for the member's own remarks since the dossier date. No agent sees another's answer. Output per member: vote, preferred path at the next three meetings, stance, confidence, dissent risk, drivers, what would change its mind, rationale in the member's register.

Aggregation

decision = modal vote among voters · split = tally · E[dissents] = Σvoters dissent_riski · P(non-unanimous) = 1 − Πvoters(1 − dissent_riski)
path = median of preferred paths (all members, dots-style) · committee lean = mean stance score · dissent base rate = share of meetings since Jan 2024 with ≥1 dissent

Calibration rules baked into the prompt: a member who has voted with the majority for a year has dissent risk ≤ 0.1 unless something specific changed; confidence above 0.9 only when the member's own words leave no doubt; no invented statements — fresh evidence must carry real URLs or be empty.

Honesty rule

The model earns its keep only when it predicts a non-unanimous outcome or a path away from market pricing. The dissent base rate is printed next to every call for that reason. Scoring against actual votes after each meeting is the next build.

Known limits

  • BoT MPC minutes are anonymised and external members rarely speak — stances are near zero with wide uncertainty by construction.
  • Some dossier facts rest on secondary sources where central-bank sites were unreachable; flagged in the dossiers.
  • Each run is a snapshot; it decays without the scheduled refresh.

A4 · Price levels and technicals

Reference levels come from the 06:37 IBKR pull (6 months daily). Per asset: 20/50/100-day simple MAs; RSI-14 (Wilder smoothing); ATR-14 from high/low/close; classic pivots (P, R1, R2, S1, S2) from the last bar; swing levels = local 3-bar highs/lows over the last 120 bars, merged when within 0.6×ATR, nearest two above/below the last price (excluding anything within 0.3×ATR); trend = up if price > 50DMA > 100DMA, down if the reverse, else range/mixed. The live column is a separate 10-minute job on free delayed quotes (~15 min, unofficial) and recomputes the same levels; it is for glancing, not for pricing a trade.

A5 · Journal and grading

Every morning call is logged with bias, entry, stop, target and the price at the call. Grading rule v0, applied at the next run: bullish wins if the next close is higher, bearish if lower, |move| < 0.05% is flat and unscored; "no trade" and "neutral" are logged but not scored. Hit rate is shown all-time and 20-day. Entry/stop/target-aware grading and holding periods are the next build.

A6 · Calendar

The morning run builds a 14-day calendar from official sources and converts every time to ICT. A required-events validator in code then checks for Thai CPI, BoT MPC, FOMC, BOJ, US CPI, NFP, PCE, Japan national and Tokyo CPI: matches are forced to high impact; misses are inserted as dashed "expected, unconfirmed" placeholders and flagged. Confirmed dates in macro/calendar/fixed.json (e.g. TPSO's published annual CPI calendar) override whatever the run guessed.
